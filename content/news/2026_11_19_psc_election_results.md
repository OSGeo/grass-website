### Corey White joins the GRASS PSC, four members re-elected

This fall, the GRASS community elected five of the nine members of the
Project Steering Committee (PSC). The open seats were those held by
**Verónica Andreo**, **Huidae Cho**, **Markus Neteler**, **Vaclav Petras**,
and **Anna Petrasova**. The community nominated *eight candidates*, and
79 of the 206 eligible voters took part in the vote, which ran
from November 2 to 9.

The PSC welcomes **Corey White** as a new member. **Verónica Andreo**,
**Huidae Cho**, **Vaclav Petras**, and **Anna Petrasova** were re-elected
for another term. Together with the four members whose seats were not open
in this election, the GRASS PSC now has the following nine members:

The full results, together with the nominations, candidate statements,
and details about the voting process, are available on the wiki page
[**PSC Election 2026**](https://grasswiki.osgeo.org/wiki/PSC_Election_2026).

We thank **Alen Mangafić**, **Edouard Choinière**, and **Brendan Harmon**
for standing as candidates. The result was close, and we are glad to have
all three of them active in the project. Our thanks also go to
**Hernán De Angelis**, who once again served as Chief Returning Officer (CRO),
and to everyone who nominated a candidate or cast a vote.

**Markus Neteler** decided not to stand for re-election and leaves the PSC,
which he has been part of since it was founded in 2006 and which he chaired
until 2020. Markus remains active in the project. Thank you, Markus,
for twenty years on the PSC!

### First meeting of the new PSC

The new PSC met for the first time on November 13. Markus Neteler
joined as outgoing member to help with the transition, and
Alen Mangafić attended as a guest to present a proposal for hosting
the 2027 GRASS Community Meeting in Ljubljana. The PSC elected
**Anna Petrasova** as its new chair. She takes over from
**Verónica Andreo**, who has chaired the PSC since 2021. Thank you, Vero!
